The [Providence Seed Library](https://provseedlib.com/){:target='_blank'} was a public seed library offering free open-pollinated, heirloom, culturally relevant, and indigenous seeds founded and organized by Fatma Maswood. Seeds were available at branches of the Community Libraries of Providence.

![](/uploads/growingguide/fatema_teaching.jpg){:style='padding: 1em 0'}

In order to make the seed library accessible to less experienced growers and gardeners, Fatma taught a series of seed starting worksops. She asked me to help design and print this Gowing Guide to collect and share that curriculum more broadly.
